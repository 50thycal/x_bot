# Content pillars

Pillar names must match `publisher/config.json` → `pillars`.

## Pre-launch mix (now)
| Pillar | Share | What it is | Example hook |
|---|---|---|---|
| build | 40% | Building in public: AI agents/tools you make (incl. this bot), systems, lessons | "I gave Grok a GitHub repo as its memory. It drafts, I merge, Actions posts." |
| design | 20% | Game-design takes: why mechanics/games work or fail | "Every mobile game I quit had the same first-5-minutes problem." |
| devlog | 15% | Early game progress: sketches, prototypes, paper playtests | "First paper prototype. 3 players, 40 minutes, one rule nobody understood." |
| community | 15% | Ask the audience to decide something | "Two box covers. Which one would you pick up off a shelf?" |
| numbers | 10% | Honest metrics: followers gained, costs, what the bot did this week | "My X bot cost $1.92 this month. Here's what it did." |
| launch | 0% | Direct CTA — off until a product link exists | — |

## Post-launch mix (switch when a game has a link)
devlog 35 · design 25 · numbers 15 · community 15 · launch 10 (max 1/week), build optional.

Rules: never two posts from the same pillar back-to-back.
