# Content pillars

| Pillar | Share | What it is | Example hook |
|---|---|---|---|
| devlog | 35% | Visible progress: GIFs, card art, before/after | "Cut 40% of the rules from my board game. Playtests got 2x faster." |
| design | 25% | Why a mechanic works or failed | "Every mobile game I quit had the same first-5-minutes problem." |
| numbers | 15% | Honest metrics: playtesters, wishlists, costs | "Printing 1 prototype copy cost $87. Here's the breakdown." |
| community | 15% | Ask the audience to decide something | "Two box covers. Which one would you pick up off a shelf?" |
| launch | 10% | Direct CTA to the bio link | "Beta is open. 50 slots. Link in bio." |

Rules: never two posts from the same pillar back-to-back; `launch` max 1/week until there is something to launch.
