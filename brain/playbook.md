# Playbook (trainable state)

> Edited ONLY through Weekly Review PRs that cite ledger evidence. Every rule has a version tag.

## Gold filter (v1)
- Keep a reply opportunity if `views / author_followers >= 12`, replies < 400, age < 6h, author or audience is in `targets.md`.
- Customer-intent posts bypass the ratio rule (any size, any views) but must be < 48h old.

## Cadence & caps (v1)
- Originals: max 3/day, ≥ 90 min apart, slots: 14:00, 17:30, 22:00 UTC (adjust to your audience's timezone).
- Daily engagement queue: 5–10 replies, ≤ 5 follow suggestions, ≤ 15 like suggestions.
- Follow cooldown: never suggest the same author twice within 14 days.

## Writing rules (v1)
- Hook in ≤ 12 words. Concrete noun in line 1.
- Media beats text for devlog posts: flag `needs_media: true` when a GIF/image would help.
